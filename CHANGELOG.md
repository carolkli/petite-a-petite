## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-19

First version.

- Hands-free sessions: it teaches a new verb and a few words, you repeat after it, then you translate English sentences out loud.
- Remembers every word and verb you've learned and schedules reviews (spaced repetition).
- Corrections come from Gemini. Exact matches are graded instantly on the phone.
- Words tab: mark anything you already know from your ChatGPT sessions.
- Export your progress to back it up or to have Claude analyze it.

**Try:** do the setup in the README, mark what you already know in Words, then go for a 10-minute run.
